Where it stands

Rev A, board saved 2026-10-02 02:22: 85 parts, 80 nets; DRC 0 violations, 0 unconnected, 0 differences from the schematic (net names apart); ERC 0; boardvis 0 errors. Not fabricated.

The part asked for was the ST60A2G0. Its ball map and antenna design are only in ST's NDA package, so rev A uses the ST60A3H1 instead: same family, public data sheet, antenna in the package, up to 480 Mbit/s (eUSB2) rather than the A2's 6.25 Gbit/s SLVS.

What is on it

  • RP2350A, core regulator, crystal, flash and decoupling as Raspberry Pi's minimal design (RP-006440), copper included
  • ST60A3H1 60 GHz transceiver; TXS0108E 1.8 V ↔ 3.3 V translator on its configuration bus, its four tunnel pins and its status lines
  • PTN3222 eUSB2 ↔ USB 2.0 repeater and two TS3USB221A switches: the USB-C connector, the RP2350's USB and the 60 GHz link, any two joined
  • 3.3 V and a switchable 1.8 V regulator (dropping it is the ST60's reset)
  • Two 1 × 16 headers 0.9 in apart, SWD on JST-SH, BOOTSEL and RUN, three LEDs
